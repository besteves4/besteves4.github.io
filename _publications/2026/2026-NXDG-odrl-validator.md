abstract: >-
  We introduce an open-source tool that helps ensure digital policies are valid, compatible, and free from conflicting rules. Focusing on W3C’s <a href="https://www.w3.org/TR/odrl-model/" target="_blank">ODRL</a>, the tool checks whether policies follow the standard and identifies potential inconsistencies that could affect how data and digital resources are used. Available both online and for integration into other applications, it makes it easier for researchers, developers, and organisations to create and exchange trustworthy policies, particularly in emerging European Data Spaces.
